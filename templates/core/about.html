About Us

CPS Legacy Chronicle

The CPS Legacy Chronicle is a digital yearbook platform designed exclusively for Computer Science students of the Class of 2026. It serves as a permanent digital archive of our academic journey, capturing the memories, achievements, and stories of each student.

Our Mission

To create a lasting digital legacy that celebrates the unique journey of every Computer Science student, preserving our collective memories for generations to come. We believe every student has a story worth telling, and this platform ensures those stories are never forgotten.
